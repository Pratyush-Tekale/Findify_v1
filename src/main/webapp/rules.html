📋 Rules & FAQ

Please read the following rules before reporting or claiming an item.

Rules

1. Report Accurate Information

Provide correct details while reporting a lost or found item.

2. Claim Only Your Item

Claim only items that genuinely belong to you.

3. Verification Required

Every claim request must pass the verification process.

4. Carry Your College ID

Bring your valid college ID while collecting your item.

5. Collect Within 30 Days

Items not collected within 30 days may be handled according to college policy.

6. Respect Privacy

Do not misuse another person's information or belongings.

7. False Claims

Providing false information may lead to rejection of your request.

8. Admin Decision

The administrator's decision regarding ownership verification is final.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a lost item?

Click on Report Lost Item, complete the form, and submit it.

How do I claim a found item?

Open the Found Items page, click Verify It's Yours, answer the verification questions, and submit your request.

How long does verification take?

Verification is usually completed within 24 hours.

Do I need my College ID?

Yes. Bring your College ID while collecting your item.

Who should I contact for help?

Use the Contact Admin page to reach the administrators.

Important Notice

Findify is a student project developed to simplify the reporting and claiming of lost and found items. Final verification and item handover are managed by the college administration.
